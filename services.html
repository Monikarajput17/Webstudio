How much does a website cost in Ludhiana?

Monika Web Studio websites start from ₹5,000. The final quote depends on pages, design depth, content, forms and integrations.

How long does it take to build a business website?

A small business website can move quickly when logo, photos, services, pricing and contact details are ready.

Do you create websites for restaurants?

Yes. Restaurant websites can include menu sections, table booking, reviews, Google Maps and WhatsApp ordering.

Can you create gym and fitness websites?

Yes. Gym websites can include plans, trainers, class schedules, trial offers, proof sections and enquiry CTAs.

Do you create school and coaching institute websites?

Yes. School and coaching websites can include admissions, batch details, faculty, notices, results and parent enquiry forms.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. Every website is responsive and built so mobile visitors can read clearly and contact you easily.

Can customers contact me through WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp buttons can be connected to your business number with a ready enquiry message.

Do you provide domain and hosting?

Domain and hosting are billed separately at actual cost, with guidance for deployment and setup.
