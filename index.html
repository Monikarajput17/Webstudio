Questions

Common questions before starting a business website.

How much does a website cost in Ludhiana?

Website pricing starts from ₹5,000 for a clean starter website. Larger business websites usually depend on pages, features, content and custom sections.

How long does it take to build a business website?

Most small business websites can be planned, designed and prepared for launch in a practical timeline once content and business details are ready.

Do you create websites for restaurants?

Yes. Restaurant websites can include menus, table booking CTAs, reviews, location details, WhatsApp ordering and photo-led sections.

Can you create gym and fitness websites?

Yes. Gym and fitness websites can show membership plans, trainers, schedules, transformation proof and quick enquiry buttons.

Do you create school and coaching institute websites?

Yes. Education websites can include admissions, courses, notices, faculty, results, parent enquiries and coaching batch details.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. Every Monika Web Studio website is built mobile-first so visitors can read, tap, call, WhatsApp and enquire comfortably from phones.

Can customers contact me through WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp CTAs can be added so customers can ask questions, book appointments, reserve tables or send enquiries directly.

Do you provide domain and hosting?

Domain and hosting charges are separate and billed at actual cost. The website files are kept easy to deploy on platforms like Netlify.
